# What I have learned: {{title}}

The diary of one first-time viewer. Write it **as that viewer**: someone from the audience in
`brief.md` who knows only what the brief says they know, and who cannot look anything up.
Go through the video in watching order, one line at a time, and write down what you now
know **in your own words** (not the script's). If you cannot say it in your own words, the
video has not taught it yet.

Rules for the diary:

- Never use knowledge the video has not given you. If a line only makes sense because you
  (the author) already know the subject, that is a finding.
- A word you were not taught is a word you do not know, however common it is among experts.
- Write "Where I got lost" honestly. Fix the script, then rewrite the entry; keep only the
  problems that are still there.
