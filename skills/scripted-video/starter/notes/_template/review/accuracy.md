## Checked for each claim

- [ ] Number, unit and date agree with the source.
- [ ] The source says this, and not something weaker ("some", "about", "in one study").
- [ ] It will still be true in a year. If not, a date is said or shown.
- [ ] Words like "always", "never", "all", "the first", "the biggest" are earned.
- [ ] "Because" is backed by the source, not only "at the same time as".
- [ ] The picture does not show more than we know (scale, direction, proportion).
- [ ] The comparison does not teach something false when pushed one step further.
- [ ] A simplified figure does not say more than the source ("more than all the money it had"
      when the source says "more than its capital").
- [ ] A real person or event is told only as the sources report it; where experts disagree about
      cause or blame, the video says so.
